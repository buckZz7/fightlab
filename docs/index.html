The Pipeline

How fighters are built

Stage 01 — Motion Tracking

Learn to move

Robots learn human combat movement from motion capture data — balance, weight transfer, and striking form.

Stage 02 — Combat Training

Learn to fight

Reinforcement learning builds fighting strategy on top of the physical foundation — punches, kicks, footwork, and defense.

Stage 03 — King of the Hill

Compete for the crown

Fighters compete in a ranked league. The top challenger earns a title shot against the reigning champion.

Stage 04 — Evolution

Always evolving

Each champion is archived as a training opponent. New challengers train against all past kings. The league never stops improving.

The Rules

How combat works

Full Combat

Punches, kicks, knees, and elbows — all legal

Head Damage x2

Head strikes deal double damage — defense matters

Kick Damage x1.5

More mass and velocity means more damage

10-Point Must

Three rounds, twenty seconds each

Draws

Close rounds scored as draws — no coin flips

King Stays on Draw

Beat the champion to take the crown
