The Pipeline

How fighters are built

01

Motion Tracking

Robots learn human combat movement from motion capture data — balance, weight transfer, and striking form.

02

Combat Training

Reinforcement learning builds fighting strategy on top of the physical foundation — punches, kicks, footwork, and defense.

03

King of the Hill

Fighters compete in a ranked league. The top challenger earns a title shot against the reigning champion.

04

Continuous Evolution

Each champion is archived as a training opponent. New challengers train against all past kings. The league never stops improving.

The Rules

How combat works

Full Combat

Punches, kicks, knees, and elbows are all legal strikes

Head Damage x2

Head strikes deal double damage — defense is critical

Kick Damage x1.5

More mass and velocity means more damage per strike

10-Point Must

Three rounds, twenty seconds each. KO, fall, or decision

Draws

Close rounds are scored as draws — no coin flips

King Stays on Draw

You must beat the champion to take the crown
