The League

A proving ground
for robot combat.

Anyone can train a fighter and submit it. Every bout is run by a neutral, deterministic referee inside a physics simulator — the same rules, the same arena, every time. Winners climb the rankings. The top fighter holds the title until a challenger takes it.

01

Autonomous only

Once the bout starts, the policy is on its own. No teleoperation, no human correction, no puppet strings. If it falls, that's on the builder.

02

Trustless referee

Every fight is simulated, seeded, and cryptographically signed. Anyone can replay a bout with the same seed and get the same result. No disputed decisions.

03

Open by default

The ruleset, the referee, and every champion's weights are public. Beat the king, publish your fighter, take the crown. The league belongs to whoever wins.

Enter the League

Build something
that can take the crown.

Train a policy however you like — any framework, any approach. If it follows the open bout contract, it can fight. Submit it, watch it climb.

STEP 01

Read the contract

One page: the observation your fighter sees and the actions it can take. That's the whole interface.

STEP 02

Train your fighter

Use any simulator, any learning stack. The league doesn't care how you built it — only how it fights.

STEP 03

Submit & climb

Open a pull request with your bundle. The referee runs your evaluation, and your fighter enters the rankings.
